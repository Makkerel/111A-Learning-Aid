> **Definition (Group).** A _group_ is a pair $(G, *)$ consisting of a set $G$ and a binary operation $*: G \times G \to G$ satisfying associativity, existence of an identity $e \in G$, and existence of inverses $a^{-1} \in G$ for every $a \in G$.

## Uniqueness & Cancellation

Even from just three axioms, we immediately get familiar algebraic rules like cancellation and unique inverses.

<details>
  <summary>Theorem: Uniqueness of Identity and Inverses</summary>

**Proof (Identity):** Suppose $e$ and $e'$ are both identity elements in $G$. Then:
$$e = e * e' = e'$$
Hence the identity element is unique.

**Proof (Inverses):** Suppose $b$ and $c$ are both inverses of $a \in G$. By associativity:
$$b = b * e = b * (a * c) = (b * a) * c = e * c = c$$
Therefore every element $a \in G$ has a unique inverse $a^{-1}$. $\blacksquare$

</details>
