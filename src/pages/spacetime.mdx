# Biquaternions & Spacetime

Unit quaternions describe rotations in $\mathbb{R}^3$. Complexifying them lets the same algebra describe transformations of **spacetime**.

> **Definition (Biquaternions):** The complexified quaternions are
>
> $$
> \mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{C}} = \mathbb{H} \otimes_{\mathbb{R}} \mathbb{C}
> $$
>
> Elements look like $q = a + b\mathbf{i} + c\mathbf{j} + d\mathbf{k}$ with $a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{C}$. The complex unit $\mathrm{i}$ commutes with $\mathbf{i},\mathbf{j},\mathbf{k}$.

## Norm and Unit Group

Quaternion conjugate and norm still make sense:

$$
\bar{q} = a - b\mathbf{i} - c\mathbf{j} - d\mathbf{k}
\qquad
N(q) = q\bar{q} = a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + d^2
$$

Now $N(q)$ need not be a nonnegative real, but multiplicativity survives: $N(pq) = N(p)N(q)$. The **complexified unit group** is

$$
U_{\mathbb{C}} = \{ q \in \mathbb{H}_{\mathbb{C}} : N(q) = 1 \}
$$

## Events in Spacetime

An event becomes a complex quaternion

$$
X = ct + \mathrm{i}(x\mathbf{i} + y\mathbf{j} + z\mathbf{k})
$$

Its norm is the Minkowski interval:

$$
N(X) = c^2 t^2 - x^2 - y^2 - z^2
$$

Write $q^\dagger$ for quaternion conjugate plus complex conjugate. For $q \in U_{\mathbb{C}}$,

$$
X \longmapsto X' = q X q^\dagger
$$

preserves the interval: $N(X') = N(X)$.

## Rotations and Boosts

A real unit quaternion gives an ordinary spatial rotation. A complex one gives a **Lorentz boost**. For example,

$$
q_B = \exp\!\left(\frac{\eta}{2}\,\mathrm{i}\mathbf{k}\right)
= \cosh\frac{\eta}{2} + \mathrm{i}\mathbf{k}\sinh\frac{\eta}{2}
$$

boosts along the $z$-axis with rapidity $\eta$:

$$
ct' = ct\cosh\eta + z\sinh\eta
\qquad
z' = ct\sinh\eta + z\cosh\eta
$$

(with $x'=x$, $y'=y$).

Just as $U \to SO(3)$ was 2-to-1, here

$$
U_{\mathbb{C}}/\{\pm 1\} \cong SO^+(1,3)
$$

— a double cover of the proper orthochronous Lorentz group. Rotations and boosts live in one quaternionic package.

> **Takeaway:** Complexify unit quaternions and the same conjugation trick that rotated $\mathbb{R}^3$ now preserves Minkowski spacetime — rotations and Lorentz boosts included.
